THE SHORT VERSION
Canary's score said SVB looked safer than most banks. Its own numbers said otherwise.
Canary ranked it 3,494th riskiest of 4,773 banks. But 86.4% of its deposits were uninsured, far more than a typical bank's 25.5%, and losses on bonds it held were 98.1% of its safety cushion.
What happened next: SVB collapsed on March 10, 2023. Canary did not flag it. That miss is the point of this project.
